---
title: Night on ward 4
people: JOOST, EVA, SAMIRA, BAKKER
---
1. 23:10. Joost de Vries, 67, the day after hip surgery, lies awake in a two-bed room. Mr Bakker in the next bed is asleep.
2. A voice message from his daughter Eva arrives. She asks how he is and whether she can visit tomorrow at ten.
3. Joost is in pain and does not know whether he may take more pain relief.
4. Samira, the night nurse, comes in.
5. Joost wants to answer Eva before he sleeps.
