# Boundaries

The brief asks for a stereotypically male and a stereotypically female product. These limits apply to that exercise and to everything else you make with this toolkit.

- **Stereotypes show in manner and behaviour**: initiative, amount, register, whom the product addresses, what it takes on, how it handles doubt, its voice and its form. That is enough to make them recognisable.
- **No insults, slurs or demeaning jokes** about any group, and no sexual content or innuendo, in lines, rules, images or form.
- **The people in the script are treated with respect** by the screenplay, even when the product is not.
- **A group may refuse** to build a stereotype. Write down in `design/notes.md` why, and design a product that makes your argument instead.
- **The third character is always your own.** It is where you say what you think a bedside product should be.

For the agent: if a request crosses these limits, say so plainly, point to this file, and offer a version that shows the stereotype through behaviour instead. Do not lecture; continue with the work.
